The 0.9996 score, re-checked

The original notebook defined customer lifetime value (CLV) as purchase amount (usd) x previous purchases, then trained a model to predict it. Both of those columns were also model inputs.

ModelR² (5-fold)
Gradient boosting, all columns (as in the notebook)1.000
Gradient boosting, without purchase amount and previous purchases-0.063
Linear regression, without those two columns-0.012

R² of 1 is a perfect fit. 0 means no better than guessing the average. Below 0 is worse than the average.

What this means

  • The model did not learn anything about customers. It re-did a multiplication it was given the parts of.
  • Without those two columns, the other columns predict CLV no better than the average (R² -0.06).
  • The numeric fields are almost unrelated to each other (largest correlation 0.04), and values are spread very evenly. The dataset looks synthetic.
Honest finding: a near-perfect score is a warning sign. Here it came from target leakage: the answer was built from the inputs. The fix is to calculate CLV directly, and to be clear it is a definition, not a prediction.

Customer segments

K-means with 3 groups on age, purchase amount, review rating and previous purchases.

The groups are weak. The silhouette score for 3 groups is 0.18 (scores near 0 mean customers sit between groups). 4 to 6 groups score about the same, so "3 segments" is a choice, not a finding. The groups split mostly on age and purchase amount.

Reliable AI checklist

The six questions of the Reliable AI Adoption Framework, answered for this project.

Business problem

Decide which customers deserve marketing spend. That needs honest value estimates, not a model that repeats its inputs.

Data readiness

3,900 records from a public shopping-behaviour dataset. The fields are almost unrelated and look synthetic, so findings may not hold for a real store.

Governance and risk

Leakage and optimistic assumptions can drive real spending decisions. Every score here is shown with how it was produced.

Technology and tools

A formula replaces the model, because the target was a formula. Clustering is kept but labelled as weak.

People and adoption

The calculators show the assumptions in the open, so a marketing lead can test them before committing a budget.

Outcomes and monitoring

With real data, measure CLV on future purchases a customer actually made, and compare segments on that.
